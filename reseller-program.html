How you earn

Two ways to earn with Ekhaya Scents

1
Share and sell

Customers order through your tracked link or use your reseller code at checkout.

2
We fulfil the order

Ekhaya Scents receives payment, packs the products and ships to the customer.

3
Refer a reseller

Earn a proposed R50 once-off reward when the referred reseller completes their first qualifying sale.

4
Get paid monthly

Approved balances are reconciled monthly and paid by EFT. Proposed minimum payout: R100.
