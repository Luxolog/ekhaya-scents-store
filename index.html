OUR STORY

Made to feel
like home.

Ekhaya means home. Our philosophy is simple: fragrance should take you somewhere — to a memory, a feeling, a person, or a version of yourself.

We craft and blend fragrances with a distinctly African point of view, while Origin brings those compositions to life through names, meanings and stories rooted in isiXhosa.
