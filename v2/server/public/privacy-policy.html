Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This page explains how Ekhaya Scents intends to handle personal information when you use our website, create an account, place an order or contact us. This draft must be reviewed and completed by Ekhaya Scents before the store is launched.

Contact: hello@ekhayascents.co.za. This policy is intended to align with applicable South African privacy requirements, including POPIA. It is not a substitute for a legal review.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the store, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, delivery and billing details, account credentials in protected form, order and transaction references, customer support messages, and preferences or consent choices.

2. How information is used

  • To create and secure customer accounts.
  • To process orders, payments, delivery and returns.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To maintain store records, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.
  • To send marketing communications only where an appropriate lawful basis and consent or permission exists.

3. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with payment providers, delivery partners, hosting and technology providers, professional advisers, or public authorities where legally required. Providers should receive only the information needed to perform their services and be subject to appropriate safeguards.

4. Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described, applicable legal requirements, and legitimate record-keeping needs.

5. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction/deletion of personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw marketing consent. You can contact us at hello@ekhayascents.co.za. You may also have rights to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

6. Cookies and website technologies

The website may use essential technologies to maintain sessions, protect forms and support account or shopping functions. Any analytics or advertising technologies should be disclosed here before activation, together with the choices available to visitors.

7. Children

The store is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without any consent or authorisation required by law.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The current version will be published on this page. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@ekhayascents.co.za.
