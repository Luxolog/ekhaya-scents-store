Return and Refund Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

At Ekhaya Scents, customer satisfaction is important to us. We take pride in providing premium-quality fragrances and home diffusers. Please review our refund and returns policy below.

Returns

We have a 7-day return policy. You may request a return within 7 days of receiving your order, but only under the following circumstances:

  • The product was damaged during delivery.
  • You received the wrong product.
  • The product is defective upon arrival.

To be eligible for a return, the item must:

  • Be unused and in its original condition.
  • Be returned in its original packaging with all accessories included.
  • Be accompanied by proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation).

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and quality assurance reasons, we cannot accept returns or exchanges on:

  • Opened or used perfumes, reed diffusers, or fragrance oils.
  • Products with broken seals (unless defective upon arrival).
  • Sale, clearance, or promotional items.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

Please include:

  • Your order number.
  • A description of the issue.
  • Clear photos of the product and packaging.

Once verified, we will arrange a replacement or refund at no additional cost.

Refunds

Once your returned item has been received and inspected, we will notify you of the outcome.

If your refund is approved:

  • Refunds will be processed to your original payment method.
  • Please allow 5–10 business days for the funds to reflect, depending on your bank or payment provider.

Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to an error by Ekhaya Scents or a damaged/defective product.

If more than 15 business days have passed since we approved your return, please contact us at hello@ekhayascents.store.
