Shipping Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

At Ekhaya Scents, we strive to deliver your order quickly, securely and affordably. We use a reliable nationwide collection service so that you can conveniently collect your parcel from your selected collection location.

Shipping Method

Orders are shipped using a nationwide collection service.

The applicable shipping fee is calculated and added at checkout.

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment has been confirmed.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

Estimated Delivery Times

  • Major Cities: 2–4 business days
  • Regional & Outlying Areas: 3–5 business days

Please note that delivery times are estimates and may vary during peak periods or due to delays outside our control.

Collection

Once your parcel has arrived at your selected collection location, you will receive a notification with the information required to collect your parcel.

Please collect your parcel within the timeframe specified in the collection notification to avoid it being returned.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email so you can monitor the progress of your shipment.

Incorrect Delivery Information

Please ensure that your contact details and selected collection location are correct before placing your order.

Ekhaya Scents cannot be held responsible for delays or failed deliveries resulting from incorrect or incomplete information supplied by the customer.

Lost or Damaged Parcels

If your parcel is lost or arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of receiving your delivery notification.

We will work with the relevant delivery service to investigate and resolve the matter as quickly as possible.
